THE OLD CAMP — VALLEY EDITION
An unofficial fan tribute inspired by Gothic. Not affiliated with THQ Nordic,
Alkimia Interactive, or Piranha Bytes. No original game assets are bundled.

ORIGINAL / GENERATED WORK
World geometry, character rigging and clothing adaptation, wildlife geometry,
quests, user interface, synthesized ambience and original musical score are
created for this project. Storm sky, initial material atlas, and transparent
pine bough artwork were generated with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool.
Prompts and workflow are recorded in assets/README.md in the source project.
